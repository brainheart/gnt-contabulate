Edition and sources

The Greek text

This explorer follows the publisher's SBL Greek New Testament v1.2, edited by Michael W. Holmes. Copyright © 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software; licensed CC BY 4.0. SBLGNT is a modern critical edition chosen for its scholarly provenance and clear permission to share the text.

Greek spelling, accents, breathings, punctuation, and editorial brackets are preserved. Book headings and apparatus pointer symbols are removed, and spacing and Unicode representation are normalized. The critical apparatus is available from the publisher.

Counts include the publisher's bracketed text of John 7:53–8:11, the shorter ending appended to Mark 16:8, and Mark 16:9–20. Omitted verses retain their gaps in the numbering, including Romans 16:25–27. No text is supplied from another edition.

The source is pinned to commit c4d241a9c1c479a55b989ba35a4976c1d0b8052c. A separate check against MorphGNT 6.12 agrees on token sequences in 7,910 verses, records differences in 17, and identifies the 12 verses added in John. The publisher's text is authoritative throughout.

Search and counting

Search ignores capitalization and elision marks, while preserving accents and breathings. For example, λόγος finds that written form; it does not automatically combine all inflections of its lemma. Acute and grave forms are distinct. Phrase counts stop at verse boundaries. Greek question marks count as sentence endings; middle dots do not.

The same historical commentary collection

Commentary comes from the HistoricalChristianFaith Commentaries Database, using the existing KJV/Luther Contabulate snapshot. Only New Testament coverage is shown. Original author names, dates, works, record IDs, and links are preserved.

Comments are mapped by passage overlap, using the following exceptions to direct verse-number matching. A merged verse counts the same excerpt once. A split verse shares passage-level comments; this does not identify which clause a commentator intended or imply that the commentator used SBLGNT.
